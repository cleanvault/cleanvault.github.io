Common Issues:

**"My license key doesn't work"**
- Make sure you're copying the entire key including "CV-PRO-"
- Check that the key hasn't expired
- Try typing the key instead of copy-pasting (sometimes hidden characters get included)

**"The tool is slow"**
- Very large PDFs (100+ MB) may be slow
- Try with a smaller PDF to confirm the tool works
- Close other browser tabs to free up memory

**"My download didn't start"**
- Check your browser's download settings
- Make sure pop-ups aren't blocked
- Try clicking the button again
