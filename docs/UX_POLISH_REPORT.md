## Terminology Consistency

### Consistent Terms

**PDF:**
- Always "PDF" (not "pdf", "Pdf", etc.)
- Always "PDFs" for plural

**Actions:**
- "Merge" (not "Combine")
- "Split" (not "Divide")
- "Extract" (not "Pull out")
- "Rotate" (not "Turn")
- "Compress" (not "Shrink")
- "Reorder" (not "Rearrange")
- "Watermark" (not "Stamp")
- "Protect" (not "Secure")

**File References:**
- "PDF file" (singular)
- "PDF files" (plural)
- "Your PDF" (possessive)

**Time References:**
- "This may take a moment." (consistent across all tools)
- "Downloading..." (consistent across all tools)
