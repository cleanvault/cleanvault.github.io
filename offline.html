How offline use works

Works after one successful visit

Once you have loaded CleanVault successfully while online, the application is saved to your browser's local cache. You can then reopen CleanVault and process PDFs with no internet connection at all.

A first visit cannot work offline

If you have never successfully loaded CleanVault before, there is nothing saved on your device to run. A complete outage prevents any first visit from starting. There is no way around this without a server or an installed app.

Your PDFs still stay local

Whether online or offline, PDFs are selected from your device and processed entirely inside your browser. They are never uploaded to any server, including ours.

Cached data can be cleared

Browsers can evict offline data under storage pressure, and private or incognito windows discard it when closed. Clearing your browsing data will remove CleanVault's saved copy.

Updates arrive when you reconnect

A cached copy may stay on an older version until you are back online, at which point CleanVault updates automatically. Purchases and the feedback form need a connection.
